Account Deletion

Last updated: August 23, 2026

You have the right to delete your Pickles and Pies account and all associated personal data at any time. This page explains exactly how to do that and what happens when you do.

1. How to Delete Your Account

From inside the app (recommended):
  1. Open the Pickles and Pies app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm your decision in the dialog that appears.
  5. Your account and data will be removed immediately.

You can also reach out to our support team at privacy@ppdeli.com and request account deletion. We will action your request within 30 days.

2. What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is removed:

  • Profile data: name, email, phone number, profile picture, language preference
  • Authentication: your Firebase Authentication record and any active session tokens
  • Order history: past orders, in-progress orders, refunds
  • Saved addresses: all delivery addresses you've added
  • Wallet & loyalty: wallet balance, loyalty points, referral balance, earned points
  • Cart & favourites: items in your cart, favourite stores/products
  • Search history: previous searches and viewed items
  • Notifications: your push-notification subscription and device token
  • Social logins: you are signed out of Google / Facebook / Apple
  • Local cache: temporary files, image cache, and SharedPreferences on your device
  • Server-side data: all rows tied to your user ID in our backend database

3. What Happens After Deletion

  • You will be signed out immediately and returned to the welcome screen.
  • Your authentication provider (Google / Facebook / Apple) is notified and your social session is revoked.
  • You will no longer receive push notifications from us.
  • Any active orders are cancelled; refunds (if applicable) are processed.
  • Once deleted, the account cannot be recovered.
⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you only want to sign out, use the "Log Out" option in Profile — it keeps your data so you can sign back in.

4. Data Retention Exceptions

Some records may be retained where required by law (for example, tax invoices, anti-fraud logs, or financial transaction records). These are retained only for the minimum period required by the applicable law and are not used for marketing.

5. Questions?
