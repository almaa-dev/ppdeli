Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Pickles and Pies ("we", "our", "us") operates a multi-vendor marketplace for food delivery, grocery delivery, eCommerce, pharmacy, parcel delivery, ride share, taxi, and home services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Delivery address and location data (with your permission)
  • Payment information (processed by certified payment processors — we do not store full card numbers)
  • Profile pictures and account preferences

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, OS version, unique device identifiers)
  • IP address, browser type, and time zone
  • App usage analytics (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Push notification tokens (via Firebase Cloud Messaging)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver orders placed through the Service
  • To facilitate communication between you, vendors, and delivery personnel
  • To send order updates, promotional offers, and service notifications
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. Sharing of Your Information

We share information with:

  • Vendors — order details and contact info necessary to fulfil your order
  • Delivery personnel — name, address, and phone number needed for delivery
  • Payment processors — to process transactions securely
  • Service providers — Firebase (analytics/messaging), cloud hosting, customer support tools
  • Law enforcement — when required by valid legal process

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard safeguards and security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and restricted access controls. Sensitive data such as authentication tokens are stored using platform-secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access & Correction — you can view and edit your profile information at any time
  • Deletion — you can request account deletion from Profile → Settings → Delete Account
  • Marketing opt-out — you can disable promotional notifications in Settings
  • Location — you can revoke location permission at any time via device settings

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The web version uses essential cookies/local storage for session management and theme preferences. We use Google Analytics (Firebase) to understand aggregate usage. You can opt out via your browser's privacy settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us to delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We take appropriate safeguards to protect your data in accordance with this Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the Policy was last revised.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@ppdeli.com
  • Address: Pickles and Pies, KSA
